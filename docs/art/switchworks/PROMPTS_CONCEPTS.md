## Atelier 17

```text
Create ONE exceptionally polished original mobile puzzle GAMEPLAY SCREEN CONCEPT for SWITCHWORKS. This is an art-direction proposal, NOT an engine screenshot and NOT an advertisement or website. Portrait phone format 9:19.5, preferably 1080x2340 or equivalent high resolution. Full bleed application screen, no phone hardware, no hand, no browser, no cards containing screenshots, no multi-panel sheet.
Keep gameplay dominant, about 65% of the screen. Use a high oblique orthographic view close enough that the physical switches can be touched easily. Three destinations in the upper portion have BOTH unambiguous symbols and colors: amber triangle A on the left, mint circle B in the middle, blue square C on the right. There is one dispatch station bottom-center with three small clearly marked parcels waiting; each parcel displays its destination symbol. A physical paired-rail route runs from this dispatch station north up a central spine. The lower interactive switch can route north along the spine or east, and the east branch bends north to destination C. The upper interactive switch on the spine can route north to B or west, and that western branch bends north to A. All three stations are physically connected by this small plausible track network. No rail can terminate into empty space. No transport vehicle carrying people; this is parcel routing. Two switches only, large thumb-sized rotating selector handles, clear branch direction chevrons. A very restrained route preview leads from source through the two switches to A. No maze spaghetti, no incoherent intersections.
Top safe-area HUD: small title "SWITCHWORKS", compact "07", clear objective "Livrer 3 colis", progress "0 / 3", discreet pause button. Bottom safe-area controls: large primary "SIMULER", secondary undo curved arrow with "Annuler", tiny "Pas à pas" option; this is the planning state, not a won screen. No lives, gems, currency, shop, ad banners, or fake social UI. Typography must be crisp, readable, carefully spaced and professional; use few labels. Keep labels upright in screen space. Use subtle material-specific UI rather than generic glass panels.
A small but legible editorial strip at the very top reads "CONCEPT ART — NON IMPLÉMENTÉ". A small footer identifies the direction by its given name. Lighting, value hierarchy, tactile material craft and depth should be exemplary. Color alone must never encode a target or parcel. No copied distinctive environment, composition, characters, logo or font from an existing game. Everything should feel manufacturable in Godot with a small studio pipeline. Render one coherent family, not a pile of unrelated assets.
DIRECTION A — ATELIER 17. An intimate precision parcel-sorting instrument, a beautiful miniature industrial workbench from an imagined postal workshop. NOT a cartoon train town. A vertically oriented warm walnut base with a deep petrol-blue enamel inset; rounded ivory station housings, satin brass paired rails and visible carefully engineered mechanical selectors. Enamel is softly reflective with tiny believable edge wear; wood grain restrained, never beneath the most important rail branches. Parcels are warm kraft-paper rectangular cartons with physical embossed address badges, neat paper bands, a small wax-colored geometric destination stamp; absolutely readable shapes. Stations are compact measuring-instrument kiosks with enamel plates and brass mouth slots, sharing exactly the same construction language. Long rails have brass highlights with dark separation and visible rail ties; they must read above the navy tabletop without glowing. Small off-board details only at edges: a clipped route ticket and one inset measuring scale, never clutter or generic toolbox props. The palette is midnight petrol #173C47, warm ivory #F2E6CC, muted terracotta #C56C47, aged brass #C8A86C and mint #77BAA0. HUD resembles carefully printed workshop instrumentation; condensed humanist sans headings and a precise monospaced counter, not steampunk ornamental typography. Primary button ivory with dark petrol text, subtly extruded mechanical key. Camera high enough to see every rail branch and the three destination symbols. Golden warm key light upper-left, controlled soft shadows and no vignette hiding the board. Footer: "A · ATELIER 17". Mature tactile premium craftsmanship, strong quiet personality, not flat vector art.
```
